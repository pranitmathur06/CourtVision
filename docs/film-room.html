What is asserted, and how well. Rows come from geometry over the ball and ten players in court feet, scored against the NBA’s own play-by-play: shots precision 0.93 / recall 0.79, rebounds 0.81 / 0.74, assists 91% cross-validated. Turnovers are the weak class — a possession change with no shot behind it is over-detected at roughly 1.8× the official count, so they carry a confidence of 0.30 and should be read as candidates, not facts. Screens, cuts and transition come from player geometry and are unvalidated against an official record, because no official record of them exists.

Why these games. SportVU optical tracking, 2015–16. The ball’s position is measured, not inferred from pixels, which is why shots here are geometry rather than computer vision.
